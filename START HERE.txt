WHATSAPP SENDER
===============

Send one message (and a photo or file if you want) to up to 200 numbers,
one by one, from YOUR OWN WhatsApp.

Works on Windows 10 and 11.


FIRST TIME
----------
1. Unzip this folder to an easy place, like your Desktop.
   Do not run it from inside the zip.

2. Double-click "start.bat".
   - If it says Node.js is missing, press Y and follow the steps.
     Then double-click "start.bat" again.
   - The first start takes about a minute. It downloads what it needs.

3. Your browser opens the WhatsApp Sender page.

4. On your phone, open WhatsApp:
   iPhone:  Settings > Linked devices > Link a device
   Android: the 3-dots menu > Linked devices > Link a device
   Then scan the QR code on the page.
   You only do this once. Next time it remembers you.


EVERY TIME
----------
1. Double-click "start.bat".
2. Paste the numbers, one per line.
   Numbers that start with 0 get the country code added (20 = Egypt).
   Change the "country code" box if your numbers are from another country.
3. Write your message. Add a photo or file if you want.
4. Click "Start sending".
5. Keep the black window open until it says it is finished.
   When it is done you can download the results.


PLEASE READ
-----------
- Messages go out from your own WhatsApp number.
- Send only to people who know you. If many people block or report you,
  WhatsApp can ban your number.
- Keep the wait between messages at 10 seconds or more.
- To unlink your WhatsApp from this PC, click "Log out" on the page.
